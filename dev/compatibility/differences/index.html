Differences from MODFLOW 6

ModflowGPU reads MODFLOW 6 input and writes MODFLOW 6 output, and its heads and budgets agree with MODFLOW 6 within the closure criteria (Comparing results). This page lists what you will notice that is different: options that are accepted but work differently, output that is not written, and behaviour of the run and of the stepping API. Unsupported input is on Supported packages.

Linear solver

The preconditioner is replaced

MODFLOW 6's ILU0, MILU0 and ILUT preconditioners work through the matrix one row after another, which does not run in parallel on a GPU. ModflowGPU replaces them with its own, chosen with the preconditioner keyword of run_simulation and initialize:

  • :auto (the default) is :mc_ilu0: ILU0 in a multicolour order (red–black on DIS grids, a greedy colouring on DISV and DISU grids), so that each colour runs in parallel;
  • :amg (smoothed-aggregation algebraic multigrid), :jacobi, :chebyshev and :mc_sgs (multicolour symmetric Gauss–Seidel).

The IMS options that configure MODFLOW 6's preconditioner (PRECONDITIONER_LEVELS, PRECONDITIONER_DROP_TOLERANCE, RELAXATION_FACTOR, and the ones that COMPLEXITY sets) are accepted, but their values are not used. The log says so when the run starts: an Info for ILU0 and for settings that come from COMPLEXITY alone, a Warning when you set RELAXATION_FACTOR or PRECONDITIONER_LEVELS yourself. MILU relaxation can be switched on with precond_options = (; relax = 0.97). The multicolour preconditioners use their own ordering: REORDERING_METHOD RCM and MD are rejected (with strict = false they have no effect, with an Info), and RED_BLACK_ORDERING is accepted and has no effect.

The convergence tests of IMS (INNER_DVCLOSE, INNER_RCLOSE with its STRICT, L2NORM_RCLOSE and RELATIVE_RCLOSE variants, OUTER_DVCLOSE) are the same, so the converged heads agree, but the number of inner iterations differs. The red–black ILU0 is a weaker preconditioner than MODFLOW 6's ILU0 in natural order and usually needs more, cheaper, iterations; the GPU guide helps to choose one.

Other differences in the linear solve

  • Each linear solve computes the head change from a zero starting guess (MODFLOW 6 starts from the current heads). The residuals, the convergence tests and the updates are the same quantities, so only iteration counts are affected.
  • The stagnation tests of CG and BiCGSTAB guard their divisions with a relative instead of an absolute tolerance. With the absolute guard, solves with tight closures could stall and run to INNER_MAXIMUM in every outer iteration; MODFLOW 6 can do that on such models, ModflowGPU stops earlier.
  • When an ILU pivot is too small, ModflowGPU keeps the unfactorised diagonal; MODFLOW 6 restarts the factorisation with a larger shift.

Outer iterations

  • Backtracking is not applied. The BACKTRACKING_* options, and the backtracking that COMPLEXITY COMPLEX turns on, are accepted with a Warning. The outer iterations then differ from MODFLOW 6's in the models where MODFLOW 6 backtracks; the converged heads agree within the closure.
  • Iteration counts differ. Because of the preconditioner and backtracking, the number of outer iterations can differ by a few, and the inner counts differ more. In :single the outer loop stops at the Float32 closures, often in fewer outer iterations than MODFLOW 6.
  • A time step that does not converge stops the run with a ConvergenceError after that step's output is written (MODFLOW 6 writes the output and stops with an error message). With the CONTINUE option in mfsim.nam ModflowGPU warns and continues, as MODFLOW 6 does.

Precision

The :double and :mixed presets give MODFLOW 6's accuracy. :single computes in Float32, which MODFLOW 6 cannot:

  • Closures are raised to what Float32 can resolve: OUTER_DVCLOSE and INNER_DVCLOSE to at least 1e-6 m (an Info message says when this happens), OUTER_DVCLOSE also to the resolution of the heads of the model (with heads 1000 m from their reference that is about 1e-4 m), and an absolute INNER_RCLOSE to the rounding level of the residual. Once a step's head change meets OUTER_DVCLOSE but its linear solve did not converge, the inner INNER_DVCLOSE is raised to the head resolution for the rest of that step.
  • Results differ from MODFLOW 6 by up to about 1e-4 m in heads, and budgets by about 1e-6 to 1e-4 of the total inflow; where conductances or storage coefficients are very large, single flows differ by more (Comparing results).
  • Some models need another preconditioner in :single (preconditioner = :amg), see the GPU guide.

Listing file

ModflowGPU writes a short model listing file with:

  • a header with the backend, the precision and the solver settings in use;
  • per time step the number of outer and inner iterations, a message when the step did not converge, and the time step length and times;
  • the budget tables, in MODFLOW 6's format, when OC prints the budget, at the end of every stress period and after a step that did not converge (as MODFLOW 6 does), so they can be read with read_listing_budgets like MODFLOW 6's;
  • the heads, when OC prints them, 10 values per line in %12.4E; HEAD PRINT_FORMAT is not applied.

It does not contain the echo of the input (PRINT_INPUT), the flow tables of PRINT_FLOWS, the solver convergence tables of IMS PRINT_OPTION, messages about cells that become dry or wet, the memory and timing reports, or the warnings and notes that MODFLOW 6 writes there. ModflowGPU reports warnings and notes in the Julia log instead.

Stepping API and BMI

The stepping API works as libmf6 and its XMI/BMI interface do, with these differences:

  • get_value_ptr returns a host array, a Float64 mirror of the device data that is copied at the calls of the time-step loop, not a pointer into the model's memory. Heads in it are absolute heads. get_value_ptr(...; device = true) returns the device array in the precision of the run, with heads as offsets from a reference head per cell.
  • Values written through get_value_ptr are sent to the device at the next prepare_time_step or prepare_solve. A write between prepare_solve and finalize_solve is discarded with a warning at finalize_solve; use set_value to change a value during the outer iterations.
  • BOUND is the package data before the AUXMULTNAME multiplier is applied. For RIV ModflowGPU keeps BOUND (stage, conductance, bottom), as earlier MODFLOW 6 versions and ModflowCoupling do; MODFLOW 6.8 has separate STAGE, COND and RBOT arrays.
  • The checks of boundary records (RIV stage and bottom, DRN elevation and GHB head not below the bottom of a convertible cell; conductances not negative) run only when period data are read. MODFLOW 6 repeats them at the start of every time step (unless NOCHECK), so it also checks values written through its API; ModflowGPU does not check values set with set_value or get_value_ptr. BOUND arrays written on the device (ModflowGPU.device_bound!) get a reduced check at prepare_solve: finite values and, for RIV, stage not below bottom and conductance not negative.

Recharge rates and older MODFLOW 6 versions

RCH rates are per unit area, and ModflowGPU multiplies them by the cell area, as MODFLOW 6 does from version 6.4 on. MODFLOW 6.3 multiplies list recharge rates by the cell area when it reads them; code that writes rates into RCH/BOUND through libmf6 6.3 (as ModflowCoupling did) must write rates per unit area for ModflowGPU.

Internal differences you may see in the data

  • A dry cell keeps its bottom elevation as head inside the engine; the head file, the host heads and get_value_ptr give HDRY (−1e30) like MODFLOW 6.
  • In :single the storage flows of convertible cells are computed from the clamped heads directly (in exact arithmetic the same as MODFLOW 6's formula), which avoids large rounding errors when cells are nearly saturated.
