Differences from MODFLOW 6

ModflowGPU reads MODFLOW 6 input and writes MODFLOW 6 output, and its heads and budgets agree with MODFLOW 6 within the closure criteria (Comparing results). This page lists what you will notice that is different: options that are accepted but work differently, output that is not written, and behaviour of the run and of the stepping API. Unsupported input is on Supported packages.

Linear solver

The preconditioner is replaced

MODFLOW 6's ILU0, MILU0 and ILUT preconditioners work through the matrix one row after another, which does not run in parallel on a GPU. ModflowGPU replaces them with its own, chosen with the preconditioner keyword of run_simulation and initialize:

  • :auto (the default) is :mc_ilu0: ILU0 in a multicolour order (red–black on DIS grids, a greedy colouring on DISV and DISU grids), so that each colour runs in parallel;
  • :amg (smoothed-aggregation algebraic multigrid), :jacobi, :chebyshev and :mc_sgs (multicolour symmetric Gauss–Seidel).

The IMS options that configure MODFLOW 6's preconditioner (PRECONDITIONER_LEVELS, PRECONDITIONER_DROP_TOLERANCE, RELAXATION_FACTOR, and the ones that COMPLEXITY sets) are accepted, but their values are not used. The log says so when the run starts: an Info for ILU0 and for settings that come from COMPLEXITY alone, a Warning when you set RELAXATION_FACTOR or PRECONDITIONER_LEVELS yourself. MILU relaxation can be switched on with precond_options = (; relax = 0.97). The multicolour preconditioners use their own ordering: REORDERING_METHOD RCM and MD and RED_BLACK_ORDERING are accepted and have no effect (for REORDERING_METHOD an Info says so). In MODFLOW 6 they only change the order of the ILU factorisation, so the number of iterations, not the converged heads.

The convergence tests of IMS (INNER_DVCLOSE, INNER_RCLOSE with its STRICT, L2NORM_RCLOSE and RELATIVE_RCLOSE variants, OUTER_DVCLOSE) are the same, so the converged heads agree, but the number of inner iterations differs. The red–black ILU0 is a weaker preconditioner than MODFLOW 6's ILU0 in natural order and usually needs more, cheaper, iterations; the GPU guide helps to choose one.

Other differences in the linear solve

  • Each linear solve computes the head change from a zero starting guess (MODFLOW 6 starts from the current heads). The residuals, the convergence tests and the updates are the same quantities, so only iteration counts are affected.
  • The stagnation tests of CG and BiCGSTAB guard their divisions with a relative instead of an absolute tolerance. With the absolute guard, solves with tight closures could stall and run to INNER_MAXIMUM in every outer iteration; MODFLOW 6 can do that on such models, ModflowGPU stops earlier.
  • When an ILU pivot is too small, ModflowGPU keeps the unfactorised diagonal; MODFLOW 6 restarts the factorisation with a larger shift.
  • With the Newton–Raphson formulation, BiCGSTAB restarts with a new shadow residual when its rho vanishes at the rounding level (a breakdown). MODFLOW 6 has no restart. It happens when the residual is nonzero in a few cells only, for example in the first outer iteration of a Newton–Raphson steady state with the red–black ILU0 (ex-gwf-zaidel), where the solve would otherwise diverge. Other models do not restart, as in MODFLOW 6.

Outer iterations

  • Iteration counts differ. Because of the preconditioner, the number of outer iterations can differ by a few, and the inner counts differ more. In :single the outer loop stops at the Float32 closures, often in fewer outer iterations than MODFLOW 6.
  • A time step that does not converge stops the run with a ConvergenceError after that step's output is written (MODFLOW 6 writes the output and stops with an error message). With the CONTINUE option in mfsim.nam ModflowGPU warns and continues, as MODFLOW 6 does.

Newton–Raphson

The Newton–Raphson formulation, its pseudo-transient continuation, NEWTON UNDER_RELAXATION and backtracking follow MODFLOW 6 (the first matrix of a run equals libmf6's), but:

  • Heads of dry cells differ. A cell whose head is below its bottom has no saturation and no outflow, and any head between the highest wet neighbour and the bottom solves its equation. MODFLOW 6 and ModflowGPU stop at different such heads (for example 1e-8 m and 1e-6 m below the bottom). Both are below the bottom, and the flows are the same.
  • Iteration counts differ more than with the standard formulation: the iterations of a Newton steady state depend on the preconditioner through the pseudo-transient continuation (ex-gwf-nwt-p03b: 96 outer iterations, MODFLOW 6 159).
  • :single may not converge on hard Newton problems: ex-gwf-nwt-p03b does not converge in Float32, while :mixed and :double do (Comparing results). Its water table lies a fraction of a millimetre above the cell bottoms in most cells, 20 m below the starting heads: in Float32 those saturated thicknesses carry only 2–3 significant digits.

XT3D

The XT3D coefficients and the matrix of the first solve equal libmf6's (to 1e-15), but:

  • With the RHS option MODFLOW 6 moves the XT3D terms of the neighbours to the right-hand side with the current heads. ModflowGPU keeps them in the residual (it solves for head corrections), with the same matrix of the direct terms: the converged heads are the same. The outer iteration counts differ, and they depend on the inner solves rather than on where the neighbour terms are: ex-gwf-hanix with XT3D RHS takes 409 outer iterations with the default preconditioner (MC-ILU0), 164 with preconditioner = :amg, and MODFLOW 6 183 (ILU0).
  • The matrix couples each cell to the neighbours of its neighbours, so on a DIS grid the multicolour ILU0 preconditioner uses more colours than the red–black ordering of the standard formulation.
  • Heads defined up to a constant. A steady-state model whose only boundaries are wells and recharge (ex-gwf-whirl) has a singular matrix: adding a constant to every head changes no flow. MODFLOW 6 and ModflowGPU end at heads that differ by such a constant (ex-gwf-whirl: 8e-6 m); the flows are the same. This holds with or without XT3D.

Several models and exchanges

The models of a solution are solved as one system with MODFLOW 6's matrix (the same entries to 1e-15 on the examples), with these differences in how it is built and reported:

  • XT3D on an exchange. MODFLOW 6 builds an interface model for each side of the exchange (the cells near it, in global coordinates) and takes the XT3D coefficients of the exchange connections, and of the connections next to them, from it. ModflowGPU computes the same coefficients directly on the grid of all models, for the same connections: the result is the same, but there is no interface model (and no GWFIM*/GWFCON* memory to read).
  • Preconditioner colouring. The multicolour preconditioners colour each DIS model red–black and keep that when every exchange connection joins two colours; otherwise (for example a refined child grid) they colour the whole solution greedily, with more colours.
  • Output. Each model's listing file has the budget table with MODFLOW 6's exchange terms and, with the exchange's PRINT_FLOWS, its flow table (EXG GWF-GWF_1 ..., in the steps whose budget OC saves, after the step's summary); the exchange rate summary that MODFLOW 6 writes to mfsim.lst is not written (there is no mfsim.lst). The budget file of a model with an exchange with SAVE_FLOWS has MODFLOW 6's FLOW-JA-FACE list of the exchange.
  • Scope. Only confined models (ICELLTYPE 0, no ICONVERT, no NEWTON) in one solution; GNC6 and MVR6 on exchanges are not supported.

Precision

The :double and :mixed presets give MODFLOW 6's accuracy. :single computes in Float32, which MODFLOW 6 cannot:

  • Closures are raised to what Float32 can resolve: OUTER_DVCLOSE and INNER_DVCLOSE to at least 1e-6 m (an Info message says when this happens), OUTER_DVCLOSE also to the resolution of the heads of the model (with heads 1000 m from their reference that is about 1e-4 m; at most 1 cm), and an absolute INNER_RCLOSE to the rounding level of the residual. Once a step's head change meets OUTER_DVCLOSE but its linear solve did not converge, the inner INNER_DVCLOSE is raised to the head resolution for the rest of that step.
  • Results differ from MODFLOW 6 by up to about 1e-4 m in heads, and budgets by about 1e-6 to 1e-4 of the total inflow; where conductances or storage coefficients are very large, single flows differ by more (Comparing results).
  • Some models need another preconditioner in :single (preconditioner = :amg), see the GPU guide.

Listing file

ModflowGPU writes a short model listing file with:

  • a header with the backend, the precision and the solver settings in use;
  • per time step the number of outer and inner iterations, a message when the step did not converge, and the time step length and times;
  • the budget tables, in MODFLOW 6's format, when OC prints the budget, at the end of every stress period and after a step that did not converge (as MODFLOW 6 does), so they can be read with read_listing_budgets like MODFLOW 6's;
  • the heads, when OC prints them, 10 values per line in %12.4E; HEAD PRINT_FORMAT is not applied;
  • the cell conversions of a model with convertible cells (CELL CONVERSIONS FOR ITER.=..., the cells that became dry or wet), as MODFLOW 6 writes them, before the iteration count of the step (those of the starting heads after the header).

It does not contain the echo of the input (PRINT_INPUT), the flow tables of PRINT_FLOWS, the solver convergence tables of IMS PRINT_OPTION, the memory and timing reports, or the warnings and notes that MODFLOW 6 writes there. ModflowGPU reports warnings and notes in the Julia log instead.

Observation files

The observation files have MODFLOW 6's header, times and number format, with these differences:

  • an observation value of zero is written as 0.000000000000000;
  • RCH and EVT observations by cell, in a model with convertible cells: at the start of a stress period that reads new recharge (evapotranspiration) data, MODFLOW 6 matches the records in the cells given in the input, ModflowGPU in the highest active cell the record was moved to. The values differ only when that cell (usually in layer 1) is dry at the start of the period; observations by boundary name are not affected;
  • the observation files of GWF–GWF exchanges are written after those of the models.

CSV files

The CSV files (BUDGETCSV, CSV_OUTER_OUTPUT, CSV_INNER_OUTPUT, AUTO_FLOW_REDUCE_CSV) have MODFLOW 6's headers, columns and number format. Their values follow from ModflowGPU's solution:

  • the budget and well-reduction values match MODFLOW 6's within the budget differences of the run (in :single, a well whose reduction is at the Float32 rounding level may have a row in one file and not in the other);
  • the outer-iteration file has the same rows where the outer iterations are the same (in :double); its inner-iteration counts, and the whole inner-iteration file, are those of ModflowGPU's linear solver, whose preconditioner differs from MODFLOW 6's (see The preconditioner is replaced): other counts, changes, residuals and step lengths. As in MODFLOW 6 the residual is the scaled one for CG and the unscaled one for BICGSTAB; after an iteration that recomputes the residual (NUMBER_ORTHOGONALIZATIONS) it is the recomputed one;
  • where several cells have the same largest change to the rounding level (a symmetric model), the cell reported may differ;
  • the package column of the outer-iteration file is always empty (no supported package has its own convergence test);
  • CSV_INNER_OUTPUT reads two values per model from the device in every inner iteration, which slows down the linear solve, most on a GPU; the other files add no device work.

Stepping API and BMI

The stepping API works as libmf6 and its XMI/BMI interface do, with these differences:

  • get_value_ptr returns a host array, a Float64 mirror of the device data that is copied at the calls of the time-step loop, not a pointer into the model's memory. Heads in it are absolute heads. get_value_ptr(...; device = true) returns the device array in the precision of the run, with heads as offsets from a reference head per cell.
  • Values written through get_value_ptr are sent to the device at the next prepare_time_step or prepare_solve. A write between prepare_solve and finalize_solve is discarded with a warning at finalize_solve; use set_value to change a value during the outer iterations.
  • BOUND is the package data before the AUXMULTNAME multiplier is applied. For RIV ModflowGPU keeps BOUND (stage, conductance, bottom), as earlier MODFLOW 6 versions and ModflowCoupling do; MODFLOW 6.8 has separate STAGE, COND and RBOT arrays.
  • The checks of boundary records (RIV stage and bottom, DRN elevation and GHB head not below the bottom of a convertible cell; conductances not negative) run when period data are read and, for a package with time series, at every time step on that step's series values. MODFLOW 6 repeats them at the start of every time step (unless NOCHECK), so it also checks values written through its API; ModflowGPU does not check values set with set_value or get_value_ptr. BOUND arrays written on the device (ModflowGPU.device_bound!) get a reduced check at prepare_solve: finite values and, for RIV, stage not below bottom and conductance not negative. With the simulation option NOCHECK these checks are skipped, as in MODFLOW 6 (the EVT PXDP checks of NSEG > 1 still run, and device BOUND values are still checked for being finite).
  • There are two BMIs. MF6Model returns what libmf6's BMI returns (one grid per model, DIS x and y as indices, libmf6's shapes and type strings), with the differences listed under Differences from libmf6; where libmf6 returns a failure status it throws. The BMI of Simulation follows the BMI specification where libmf6's BMI does not: settings come from a configuration file, the grid functions give real-world coordinates of the cell centres (DIS) or of the vertices (DISV, DISU), every variable has a grid (scalars and package arrays too), and the units are UDUNITS strings. See BMI.

Recharge rates and older MODFLOW 6 versions

RCH rates are per unit area, and ModflowGPU multiplies them by the cell area, as MODFLOW 6 does from version 6.4 on. MODFLOW 6.3 multiplies list recharge rates by the cell area when it reads them; code that writes rates into RCH/BOUND through libmf6 6.3 (as ModflowCoupling did) must write rates per unit area for ModflowGPU.

Internal differences you may see in the data

  • A dry cell keeps its bottom elevation as head inside the engine; the head file, the host heads and get_value_ptr give HDRY (−1e30) like MODFLOW 6.
  • In :single the storage flows of convertible cells are computed from the clamped heads directly (in exact arithmetic the same as MODFLOW 6's formula), which avoids large rounding errors when cells are nearly saturated.
