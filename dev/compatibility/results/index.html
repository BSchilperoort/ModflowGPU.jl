Comparing results

ModflowGPU solves the same equations as MODFLOW 6, so on the same input it should give the same heads and budgets, up to the closure criteria of the solver and the rounding of the chosen precision. This page says how close the results are, how that is checked, and how to check it on your own model.

What is compared

Every tested model is run with MODFLOW 6.8.2 and with ModflowGPU, and the results are compared:

  • Heads: every saved time step of the head file, every cell.
  • Budgets: every record of the budget file (the flows between cells, storage and each package's flow per boundary cell) and every term of the budget tables in the listing file (rate in and rate out per package), per time step. Differences are given relative to the total inflow of the time step.
  • Percent discrepancy of each budget table.

Both programs run with tight closures (OUTER_DVCLOSE 1e-9, INNER_DVCLOSE 1e-10, INNER_RCLOSE 1e-10, with enough iterations), so that each result is close to the exact solution of the equations and the comparison measures the equations, not where each solver happened to stop. With the loose closures of production runs (an OUTER_DVCLOSE of metres) both programs stop early, at different places, and their heads differ by up to the closure.

The preconditioner is ModflowGPU's default, not MODFLOW 6's ILU (see Differences from MODFLOW 6), so the iteration counts differ. Iteration counts are reported, not compared.

Acceptance limits

A model passes when all of these hold:

Comparison:double and :mixed:single
Heads against MODFLOW 6max |Δh| < 1e-6 m (or 1e-7 × the head range, if larger)max |Δh| < 1e-3 m
Budgets against MODFLOW 6< 1e-6 × total inflow, per record and per listing term< 1e-4 × total inflow; percent discrepancy < 0.01 %
:single against :double–max |Δh| < 1e-3 m
GPU against the CPU backendheads within 1e-10 × the largest |head|heads within 1e-4 m

:mixed keeps the heads and the solver vectors in Float64 and only runs the preconditioner in Float32, so it must meet the :double limits.

Why :single has wider limits, in plain terms:

  • Rounding. A Float32 number has about 7 significant digits. ModflowGPU stores each head as a difference from a reference head of its cell, so the 7 digits go to the part that changes; still, with heads that vary by hundreds of metres within a model, a Float32 head is only resolved to about 1e-5 to 1e-4 m.
  • Closures. For the same reason the solver cannot meet closures below that resolution. In :single, closures tighter than what Float32 can reach are raised (to at least 1e-6 m for OUTER_DVCLOSE and INNER_DVCLOSE, more for models with a large head range), with an Info message in the log. The results are then as close to MODFLOW 6 as the rounding allows.
  • Budgets. Flows are conductance × head difference. Where conductances or storage coefficients are very large, the rounding of the heads is multiplied by them. For five models (rad_disu_confined, ex-gwf-rad-disu, ex-gwf-maw-p03c, ex-gwf-advtidal, ex-gwf-zaidel) the :single budget limit is applied to the part of the difference that exceeds this rounding bound, computed per flow; the raw differences are larger (on the radial DISU grid, up to 0.4 × total inflow in single flows). Use :mixed or :double when budgets of such models matter.
  • Iterations. Not all models converge in :single with the default preconditioner. ex-gwf-lgrv-gr is run with preconditioner = :amg in :single (see the GPU guide). The Newton–Raphson example ex-gwf-nwt-p03b does not converge in :single at all and is tested in :mixed and :double only.
  • Newton–Raphson. Cells whose head is below their bottom are dry, and their head is not unique (Differences from MODFLOW 6): the comparison checks that such cells are below their bottom in both runs and leaves their heads out of the differences.

Three models use a criterion of their own, all for documented reasons in the developer notes: ex-gwf-rad-disu in :double and :mixed (budgets within 1e-5 × total inflow: one storage flow in a step of 8 seconds amplifies a 2.5e-10 ft head difference), and ex-gwf-hwell-a, ex-gwf-rad-disu and rad_disu_confined, which run with preconditioner = :amg in every preset.

Large models: the Indus basin and the global 5′ model

ModflowGPU was built for coupled runs with a hydrological model, so it was also compared with MODFLOW 6 on the models it was made for. These comparisons use the coupling quantities (baseflow to rivers and capillary rise) as well as heads, with tight closures (INNER_RCLOSE 1e-8, as MODFLOW 6 does not converge at 1e-10 on these models), on the ROCm backend (on the Indus the CPU backend gives the same digits).

Indus basin (204 × 180 cells in 2 layers; RIV, DRN, RCH, EVT, CHD, STO; three monthly stress periods, and 31 daily ones), against MODFLOW 6.3.0 (the version the coupling uses). Flows are the largest difference relative to the largest value in the domain:

Per day MODFLOW 6 needed 7 outer and 14–15 inner iterations; ModflowGPU 7 and 15 (5 and 9 in :single).

At production closures (OUTER_DVCLOSE of 5 m) MODFLOW 6 stops up to 1.9 m from its own tight-closure heads on monthly Indus periods, while ModflowGPU stops within 1e-5 m of them. The two programs then differ visibly, and the difference is MODFLOW 6 stopping early, not a difference in the equations. With daily periods at the same closure both stop about 0.15 m from the tight heads, and agree with each other to 1e-6 m.

Check it yourself

Run MODFLOW 6 and ModflowGPU on the same simulation and compare the output files with the readers of ModflowGPU, which read the files of both programs. Use tight closures in the IMS file of both runs (or pass closures to run_simulation) if you want to compare the equations rather than the stopping points.

using ModflowGPU

simdir = "path/to/simulation"          # MODFLOW 6 has run here: model.hds, model.cbc, model.lst
r = run_simulation(simdir; backend = select_backend("auto"), outdir = mktempdir())

# Heads: the largest difference over all saved steps
ours, mf6 = open_head_file(r.head_file), open_head_file(joinpath(simdir, "model.hds"))
dh = maximum(maximum(abs.(read_head(ours, k) .- read_head(mf6, k))) for k in 1:length(mf6))

# A package's flows in the last saved step
a, b = open_budget_file(r.budget_file), open_budget_file(joinpath(simdir, "model.cbc"))
ia = last(ModflowGPU.find_budget_records(a; text = "RIV"))
ib = last(ModflowGPU.find_budget_records(b; text = "RIV"))
dq = maximum(abs.(read_budget(a, ia).q .- read_budget(b, ib).q))

# The listing budget tables: rate in and out per package, and the percent discrepancy
ModflowGPU.listing_rates(r.listing_file, "RIV"), ModflowGPU.listing_rates(joinpath(simdir, "model.lst"), "RIV")
[t.rate_percent_discrepancy for t in read_listing_budgets(r.listing_file)]

Dry cells hold the same HDRY value in both head files; leave them out of the difference. The readers are described on Output files: open_head_file, read_head, open_budget_file, find_budget_records, read_budget, read_listing_budgets, and listing_rates.
