`T6.2-newton` against `main` (d0ba506), back to back, no other GPU job:

```sh
julia -t 8 --project=benchmark benchmark/run.jl rocm global5m precision=double,mixed,single precond=mc_ilu0 timed=0 ims=parity
```

global5m is not a Newton model: the Newton terms, the pseudo-transient continuation and
NEWTON UNDER_RELAXATION are not formulated (`jac = false`), and the BiCGSTAB breakdown
check (`coupling`, BiCGSTAB) costs no reduction (it uses the `rho` that BiCGSTAB already
reduces). Iteration counts, head differences between the presets and peak memory equal
`main`'s; the time differences are run-to-run noise (±1–2 %).
