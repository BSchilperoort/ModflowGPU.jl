# T5.5a: global-size dress rehearsal

Measured 2026-11-14 on the RX 7800 XT (16 GiB, 17,163,091,968 bytes; ROCm 7.2.2,
Julia 1.12.6, KA 0.10.7, AMDGPU 2.11.0), branch `T5.5a-global-rehearsal`, ModflowCoupling
patches 0001–0005, mGV `main` 42aebaa (unchanged since T5.4). The desktop used 915–932 MiB
of the card before every run (sysfs `mem_info_vram_used`); every number below is net of it.

## What ran

`examples/mgv_coupling/global_rehearsal.jl` (patch 0005), one process per row, both
models on rocm:

1. mGV with its **real global inputs**: `vic_global_5min_params_lossless.nc` (2,288,857
   active cells, 7.4 M tiles), the generated routing file (below), the land-only WFDE5
   v3.1 forcing of 1979 (`docs/global_inputs.md`), `external_groundwater_routing = true`,
   no output files. Config: `examples/global/mgv_global.toml`.
2. MODFLOW: the **synthetic `global5m`** of T3.4 (4320 × 2160 × 2, 6,592,790 nodes) put
   through ModflowCoupling's parameter code and GPU engine **from arrays**
   (`StaticInputs`, `GroundwaterModel(config, inputs)`; no NetCDF): ModflowCoupling's
   package set, so RIV, DRN, EVT and RCH each have a record on every land cell
   (3.2 M records each; the synthetic model of T3.4 splits land between RIV and DRN),
   IMS as ModflowCoupling (BiCGSTAB, COMPLEX, SIMPLE under-relaxation). Its grid is the
   global 5′ grid, so mGV's cells map onto it by centre (`cell_maps`).
3. **Steady-state spin-up** (`period = "steady"`, synthetic mean recharge and
   0.3 × bankfull discharge, spin-up settings of `spinup_steady_state.jl`: 2000 outer
   iterations, γ = 0.5, closure 0.01), then the spin-up model is finalized and the daily
   model built from its heads.
4. **365 coupled days** (1979): mGV day D with MODFLOW's feedback, MODFLOW day D with
   mGV's recharge (device exchange, gather + conversion kernels) and a synthetic
   seasonal river discharge (mGV's discharge is gathered on the device too, but not used:
   with it MODFLOW did not converge on day 2, see "mGV's routing blows up" below),
   baseflow and capillary rise back to mGV.

The land masks of the two models differ, so the exchanged fields mean nothing
physically; the run measures memory and time. Times are medians from day 3 (synchronised;
day 1 compiles). The whole year ran in every row: nothing is extrapolated.

The `:single` rows with "(amg)" ran their spin-up with AMG (an interim rule of this card,
reverted, see below); their daily steps use MC-ILU0 like the others. Spin-up heads
against `:double`: `:mixed` 2.7e-7 m, `:single` with MC-ILU0 1.5e-5 m, with AMG 9.4e-4 m.

**Closures at global size** (decision 9): on `global5m` closures 5 and 0.01 cost the same
(2 outer and 3 inner iterations per day in both; the synthetic daily changes are small), so
0.01 is free here; 1e-3 adds a third outer iteration on most days (+41 % MODFLOW time in
`:mixed`, +30 % per coupled day) for heads 1.3e-5 m from those at 0.01. On the Indus 0.01
cost 1.46× of 5 (T5.4); the real global model (T5.5b) is the one to recheck, since the
synthetic steady state is well conditioned (T3.4).

**mGV against MODFLOW (T4.7).** Per coupled day at the default (`:mixed`, 0.01) mGV takes
34.2 ms and MODFLOW plus the exchange 91.6 ms: **MODFLOW is 73 % of the day (ratio
mGV : MODFLOW = 0.37), so at global size ModflowGPU limits the coupled daily step, and by
T4.7's rule T4.7 is needed** (on the Indus mGV dominated: 1.70 vs 1.20 ms). Even in
`:single` MODFLOW is 62 % (57.2 vs 34.6 ms). A coupled year takes 46 s (`:mixed`) plus
about 2 min of mGV initialisation and 16 s of spin-up.

## mGV's routing blows up under the coupling feedback (review finding)

Found in the review (`:mixed`, closure 0.01, rocm). The day-2 failure with mGV's discharge
is not a stage-geometry artefact of the synthetic channels: mGV's own discharge is
broken. On day 2, after the first MODFLOW baseflow (max 7.7 m³/s per cell) and capillary
rise are applied, mGV's `channel_water__volume_flow_rate` reaches 8.5e37 m³/s with 58
non-finite cells (Greenland and Arctic cells first, e.g. 59.8 W 76.5 N); 95 non-finite and
440–912 cells above 1e5 m³/s by days 30–60. On the wide RIV cells this gave stages up to
3e20 m and 200 non-converged outer iterations. mGV alone on the same inputs (no feedback)
stays finite all of 1979 (max 6.0e4 m³/s). Likely cause (not proven): mGV's explicit
kinematic wave (600 s sub-steps, celerity up to 10 m/s at the 6 m/s velocity cap) is
unstable on short channels; the routing file has 74,985 cells with a channel length below
3 km (high latitudes; minimum 1.03 km), and the baseflow pushes water into them. The
accepted runs above carry this broken mGV routing state (the synthetic discharge hides it
from MODFLOW); memory and the cost per day are not expected to depend on it, but mGV's
34 ms per day was measured on it. **T5.5b feeds mGV's discharge to MODFLOW and will meet
this**: mGV is not patched here, so it is an owner decision (smaller or adaptive routing
sub-steps in mGV, or a minimum channel length in the routing file) before T5.5b.

## The steady-state spin-up in `:single` (T6.0 bullet)

Decision: `:single` keeps the default MC-ILU0 for the spin-up (no automatic AMG), and the
STRICT INNER_RCLOSE floor stays eps32·max Σ|terms| (no head-rounding term).

- On `global5m` (ModflowCoupling's IMS, closure 0.01) MC-ILU0 in Float32 converges in
  21 outer / 325 inner iterations, 16.1 s, 1.5e-5 m from `:double`; AMG takes 41.5 s
  (16 / 87) and stops 9.4e-4 m away, with a MODFLOW peak of 4027 MiB against 3613 MiB.
  An automatic AMG for `:single` steady states (tried here as ModflowCoupling's
  `"auto"`) was therefore reverted.
- The failure of T6.0 (ex-gwf-lgrv-gr, `:single`, MC-ILU0, STRICT: no convergence) shows
  as a non-converged step (ModflowCoupling raises an error), not as wrong heads, so the
  remedy is explicit: `preconditioner = "amg"` for that spin-up, or `:mixed` (the `:auto`
  precision on backends with Float64, where MC-ILU0 is fine on lgrv-gr). Documented in
  ModflowCoupling's `initialize_gpu` and `docs/precision.md`.
- On the Indus (real inputs, ModflowCoupling's spin-up settings, CPU backend) Float32
  stops about 0.03 m from the converged heads whatever the preconditioner and closure
  (closure 0.01: MC-ILU0 0.072 m, AMG 0.032 m, `:double` itself 0.028 m; closure 1e-6:
  MC-ILU0 and AMG 0.032 m, `:mixed` 9.3e-10 m; `docs/precision.md`). So the spin-up of
  T5.5b should run in `:mixed` (the `:auto` default on rocm); AMG is not the fix for
  `:single` there.
- A head-rounding rclose floor made lgrv-gr converge 6.1e-3 m from the exact heads (T6.0):
  it turns a visible failure into a silent error, so it is not adopted.
