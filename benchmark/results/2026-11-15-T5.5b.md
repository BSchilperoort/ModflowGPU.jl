# T5.5b: global 5′ coupled run with real data

Measured 2026-11-15 on the RX 7800 XT (16 GiB; ROCm in `/opt/rocm`, Julia 1.12.6,
KernelAbstractions 0.10.7, AMDGPU 2.11.0), branch `T5.5b-global-run`, ModflowCoupling
patches 0001–0006, **mGV `main` at 42aebaa** (unchanged since T5.4; not patched). Both
models on rocm, MODFLOW in `:mixed` with the default `:mc_ilu0`. The desktop used
2.45–2.54 GiB of the card before each run (sysfs `mem_info_vram_used`); memory numbers
below are net of it unless marked.

Driver: `examples/global/run_global.jl` of the patched ModflowCoupling (patch 0006; usage in
`examples/global/README.md` there). Derived inputs: `docs/global_inputs.md`, "Derived inputs
of the global run".

## Summary

- **The run completed on the 16 GB GPU**: 200 years of coupled spin-up (73,000 coupled
  days) and then 365 coupled days from the spun-up state, plus 60-day runs at closures 5,
  0.01 and 1e-3. Highest card use: 7.06 GiB net (9.5 GiB with the desktop) during the
  spin-up; pool peak 5229 MiB.
- **Created water: 0 m³ on every day** of every run (spin-up and daily runs), with the
  river-loss limit below. mGV's discharge was finite on all 73,000 + 545 days, at most
  256,290 m³/s (Amazon-scale).
- **The stopping criterion was not met in 200 years**: the run stopped at the year limit.
  Baseflow and storage levelled off; the head drift did not reach its thresholds (layer 1
  mean |Δh| 0.0134 m/yr, p99 0.252 m/yr at year 200; drift series below).
- Forcing: **the 1979 land-only store, cycled** (the fallback). mGV `main` cannot read the
  gridded store correctly (it ignores the int16 scale, offset and fill value; below).

## River-loss limit (design)

MODFLOW gets the water in each channel after mGV's day D (`river_water_volume` = mGV's
cross-section × channel length, gathered on the device), and raises the RIV bottom of every
record to `rbot' = max(rbot, stage − 0.999·V/(C·days))`. A RIV cell loses at most
`C·(stage − rbot')` per day whatever the head, so its loss over the period is at most
0.999 V; gains (head above the stage) are unchanged, and with no volume set (Inf) or a
volume above the potential loss the records are exactly the original ones. mGV applies that
loss on day D+1 to exactly that channel water. Implemented in ModflowCoupling
(`river_bottom`, host and device paths; the bottoms as read are restored where the limit
does not apply). The coupling example also sets mGV's discharge to mGV's Manning discharge of
the new cross-section after adding the baseflow (`channel_discharge`): otherwise mGV's next
routing sub-step releases `Q·dt` computed for the old (larger) area and its clamp at 0
creates water.

Chosen: **the limit is on by default in the coupling example and switched off in
ModflowCoupling unless a volume is set**, so the existing parity results are unchanged
(they set no volume) and BMI users that do not know the variable get the old behaviour.
Tests (Indus, 2 daily periods, mf6 and GPU `:single`/`:double` with device and host
coupling): unset, Inf and 1e30 give bitwise the same heads and RIV flows; with half the
unlimited loss as volume on the 853/879 losing cells every loss is ≤ the volume (largest
margin −15.4 and −0.64 m³) and the total loss on those cells is 0.5× the unlimited one (not
switched off); dry channels give no loss and keep the gains.

Created water per day (clamp at 0 in the coupling's baseflow kernel, accumulated in
Float64 per cell): **0 m³ on every one of the 73,000 spin-up days and the 545 daily-run
days.** mGV's own routing clamp is not instrumented (mGV is not patched); with the discharge
consistent with the area and every channel ≥ 4639 m, a sub-step moves at most
6·600/4639 = 0.78 of a channel's water, so it is not reached by construction (not measured).

## Spin-up: 200 years, stopping criterion not met

Start: the provided steady head (`initial_head_gv2p41_5min_nogl.nc`, CHD ring 0 m), mGV
cold (its initial soil moisture, empty channels). No steady-state solve was attempted
(owner decision 2026-11-15; the previous attempt did not converge). Checkpoints every 10
years (MODFLOW heads as NetCDF in the `initial_head_file` layout, mGV's state arrays, the
coupling's feedback; 1.0 GiB each); **a restart from a checkpoint is bitwise identical**
to the continuous run (year 3 from the year-2 checkpoint: heads max |Δ| = 0, daily log
identical). mGV `main` has no save/restore: the driver copies every array of mGV's state
structs (surface energy, canopy, soil, snow, routing area/discharge) and its clock.

**Stopping criterion** (defined from the 3-year short run, before the long run; year
against the previous year, all four for 5 consecutive years): layer-1 mean |Δh| <
0.01 m/yr, layer-1 99th percentile |Δh| < 0.1 m/yr, |relative change of the annual baseflow|
< 0.1 %/yr, |MODFLOW storage change over the year| < 1 % of the annual recharge. Short-run
numbers it was set against (year 3): 0.215 m/yr, 3.19 m/yr, 26 %/yr, 33 %.

**Outcome: the year limit (200), not the criterion.** Baseflow met its threshold from year
27 and the storage change from year 112; the head drift did not: at year 200 layer-1 mean
|Δh| is 0.0134 m/yr (mean Δh +0.0034, heads still rising) and p99 0.252 m/yr. Estimate
(power-law extrapolation of years 100–200, **an estimate, not a run**): mean |Δh| < 0.01
after about 340 years, p99 < 0.1 after about 1200 years.

## Deviations and open issues

- Forcing: the 1979 land-only store cycled instead of the gridded 1979–2024 record (mGV's
  gridded reader, above). Owner: fix in mGV, or land-only stores of more years.
- The spin-up did not meet the stopping criterion in 200 years (heads still rising
  0.0034 m/yr on average, p99 0.25 m/yr); baseflow and storage did. Remaining: about 140
  more years for the mean, far more for p99 (estimates).
- No steady-state solve was tried (the transient spin-up was the owner's choice).
- The forcing cycle and the mGV checkpoint use mGV internals (`clock`, state structs,
  `update_forcing!`, `load_monthly_parameters!`, `routing.alpha`), not its BMI; they break if
  mGV changes those.
- The exchange is large in both directions: 79 km³/day of baseflow against 23 km³/day of
  river loss and 57 km³/day of recharge at year 200; plausibility not judged here.
- 60 mGV cells south of 60°N have no MODFLOW land cell (no feedback), as in T5.5a.
- Not verified: mGV's internal routing clamp (not instrumented); the cause of the single
  card peak of 7.2 GiB.
