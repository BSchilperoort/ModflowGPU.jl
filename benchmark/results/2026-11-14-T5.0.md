# T5.0: KernelAbstractions 0.10 as the minimum

Measured 2026-11-14 on the machine of `2026-11-07.md` (RX 7800 XT, Julia 1.12.6), branch
`T5.0-ka010-minimum` (KA 0.10.7, KernelInterface 0.4.6, AMDGPU 2.11.0, GPUArrays 12.2.0,
GPUCompiler 2.22.2), benchmark lock held. Same-session baseline: `main` at b9c97eb with
the Phase 4 manifest (KA 0.10.5, KernelInterface 0.4.5, AMDGPU 2.11.0, GPUCompiler 2.21.0).

```sh
julia -t 8 --project=benchmark benchmark/run.jl rocm indus global5m precision=double,mixed,single precond=jacobi,mc_ilu0 timed=1
```

Models, closures and columns as in `2026-11-08-T4.5.md` (steady + 30 daily steps,
`synthetic_ims(:parity)`).

## What changed

| Change | Where | Results |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KA 0.10.5 → 0.10.7 (compat `0.10`), GPUCompiler 2.21 → 2.22 | `Project.toml` | bitwise |
| Reduction tree as a loop in a helper (`_tree_reduce!`) instead of 8 written-out levels per kernel | `src/solver/krylov.jl` | bitwise (same pairwise order) |
| No `applicable(unsafe_wrap, …)` check in `host_mapped_zeros` / `pin_memory!` | `ext/AMDGPUExt.jl` | bitwise |

Bitwise check: heads after the steady step and 4 daily steps, and outer and inner
iterations of every step, for `tiny` and `indus` × `:double`, `:mixed`, `:single` ×
Jacobi, MC-ILU0, AMG (18 cases), on rocm and on the CPU backend (POCL): identical to `main`
on KA 0.10.5, both with the new code on KA 0.10.5 and on KA 0.10.7. In the table below the
iterations and Δh columns are identical to `main` and to T4.5 in every row.
