# T5.6: global parity and timing against MODFLOW 6

Measured 2026-11-15 on the RX 7800 XT (16 GiB, ROCm in `/opt/rocm`) and the Ryzen 7 7700
(8 cores, 16 threads, 61 GB), Julia 1.12.6, KernelAbstractions 0.10.7, AMDGPU 2.11.0,
branch `T5.6-global-mf6-parity`, ModflowCoupling patches 0001–0007 (0007 adds the drivers
only; no change in ModflowCoupling's `src/` or `test/`, none in ModflowGPU's `src/`), mGV
`main` at 42aebaa (not patched). MF6 = the mf6 engine of ModflowCoupling, i.e. **libmf6
6.3.0 from `Modflow6_jll`** (ModflowInterface), not the 6.8.2 build in `reference/`.

Drivers: `examples/global/parity_mf6.jl` and `parity_compare.jl` of the patched
ModflowCoupling (usage in `examples/global/README.md` there). Outputs:
`/home/bart/data/mGV/global_60N/runs/T5.6/`.

## Summary

- **The GPU engine matches MF6 on the real masked global model within the T5.3 tolerances
  in every preset**, 31 daily steps at tight closures, open loop (table below):
  `:mixed` and `:double` max |Δh| 4.4e-11 m (tolerance 1e-4 m), baseflow 3.6e-12 and
  capillary rise 5.3e-12 of the domain maximum (tolerance 1e-6); `:single` 2.5e-5 m
  (1e-3 m), 1.2e-5 and 3.9e-6 (1e-4).
- **MF6 ran the full model as specified**: all 3,675,450 nodes, 31 daily periods, tight
  closures (OUTER_DVCLOSE 1e-9, INNER_DVCLOSE 1e-10, INNER_RCLOSE 1e-8 STRICT), river-loss
  limit on; it converged every day (7 outer / 14–15 inner iterations). Nothing reduced.
- **Budgets** of both engines agree to 1e-12 relative per term and day (`:mixed`,
  `:double`) and 1e-6 (`:single`).
- **River-loss limit**: MF6 and the GPU engine in `:mixed`/`:double` both follow the
  formula of T5.5b (flux = C·(stage − max(h, rbot′)), rbot′ = max(rbot, stage −
  0.999·V/(C·days))) to 2.8e-7 / 3.5e-7 relative, and no record loses more than 0.999 V.
  `:single` exceeds the volume on 117 record-days (322 m³ in 31 days, max loss/volume
  1.0073): the Float32 known issue from the T5.5b review, not an engine difference.
- **Speed** (MODFLOW per daily step, medians of days 3–31, start-up excluded): GPU on rocm
  **48–89×** MF6 at equal closures (`:mixed` 56–60×), **22–37×** at each engine's
  production closure (MF6 5 against GPU 0.01); GPU engine on the CPU backend (16 threads)
  6.0–10× MF6. MF6 is serial: the 6.8.2 build in `reference/` has no MPI ("Can not run
  parallel mode with this executable: no MPI"), so no parallel MF6 number. The speed-ups
  are against serial libmf6 6.3.0 with ModflowCoupling's COMPLEX IMS; the 6.8.2 executable
  is not faster, and with ILU0 and no backtracking it is a third faster than with COMPLEX,
  which brings the `:mixed` speed-up at 0.01 to about 48× (review checks, end of file).
  At the production pair the MF6 heads are 0.51 m from converged, the GPU heads 4e-9 m.

## Method

1. **Record** (`mode=record`): the coupled run of T5.5b's `run_global.jl mode=daily` from
   `checkpoint_y0200` (year-200 heads and mGV state), mGV and MODFLOW on rocm, device
   exchange, GPU engine `:mixed` at closure 0.01, river-loss limit on, 31 days from
   1979-01-01. Each day the three fields mGV hands to MODFLOW are written as mGV holds
   them (Float32 on its 1,713,802 cells): discharge (m³/s), subsurface runoff (mm/day) and
   channel volume (m³), with the mGV → MODFLOW cell map (637 MB). Created water: 0 m³ on
   every day. The record run took 25 ms (mGV) + 65 ms (MODFLOW) per day, as T5.5b.
2. **Replay** (`mode=replay`): MODFLOW alone, built from the same heads, each day's fields
   set with `set_value_device!` and the recorded map: on the device for the GPU engine, on
   the host path of `set_value_device!` for the mf6 engine (same Float32 division by 1000
   and conversion, so both engines get the same Float64 values). The steps of
   `advance!` are called one by one with timers.
3. **Replay check**: the GPU `:mixed` replay at 0.01 reproduces the recorded coupled run
   **bitwise** (heads, baseflow and capillary rise of all 31 days, `cmp`).
4. **Compare** (`mode=compare`): per day, max |Δh| over the active nodes of both layers;
   baseflow and capillary rise as max |Δ| over the land cells / max |reference| over the
   land cells (the T5.3 metric); location, water-table depth (model top − reference head of
   layer 1) and boundary records of the worst cell; budgets per term (GPU: its budget;
   MF6: package SIMVALS sums for RCH, RIV, DRN, EVT and the listing for storage and CHD,
   because MF6's CHD SIMVALS hold the net flow per CHD cell and differ from its listing
   budget by up to 6 %).

## Parity (31 daily steps, tight closures in both engines)

Against MF6 at tight closures; max over the 31 days. Tolerances of T5.3: heads 1e-4 m,
fluxes 1e-6 (`:double`, `:mixed`); 1e-3 m, 1e-4 (`:single`, Float32 floors of the closures).

| GPU engine (rocm) | max \|Δh\| | baseflow | capillary rise | p99 \|Δh\| | nodes > 1e-4 m | outer / inner per day (GPU; MF6 7 / 14–15) | tolerance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `:mixed` | 4.4e-11 m | 3.6e-12 | 5.3e-12 | 6.8e-12 m | 0 | 7 / 15 | 1e-4 m; 1e-6 |
| `:double` | 4.4e-11 m | 3.6e-12 | 5.3e-12 | 6.8e-12 m | 0 | 7 / 15 | 1e-4 m; 1e-6 |
| `:single` | 2.5e-5 m | 1.2e-5 | 3.9e-6 | 1.9e-7 m | 0 | 5 / 9 | 1e-3 m; 1e-4 |

Margins: 2.3e6 (heads), 2.8e5 (baseflow) for `:mixed`/`:double`; 40 and 8 for `:single`.
`:mixed` and `:double` are not bitwise equal (they differ in the last bits) but have the
same maxima.

**Growth over the month** (max |Δh| on days 1, 10, 20, 31): `:mixed` 3.6e-12, 1.6e-11,
2.9e-11, 4.4e-11 m; `:single` 5.0e-6, 1.6e-5, 2.2e-5, 2.5e-5 m. Both grow, roughly linearly
for `:mixed` (rounding accumulated per step) and flattening for `:single`; at these rates
a year of daily steps stays orders of magnitude inside the tolerances (extrapolation, not
a run).

**Where the largest differences are:**

- `:mixed`/`:double` heads: the Andes of southern Peru (−71.4°, −15.1° on days 16–31,
  −70.7°, −15.5° on days 7–13, −75.1°, −13.2° on day 28), deep water tables (164–508 m
  below the model top), cells with a drain-like RIV record, DRN and EVT; often layer 2.
  Baseflow: the Tibetan plateau (84–101° E, 28–34° N), drain-like RIV + DRN cells,
  2e-10 to 4e-10 m³/s on 100–245 m³/s.
- `:single` heads and baseflow: the lower Amazon (−52° to −61°, −1.4° to −3.4°), cells with
  a wide river (RIV), DRN and EVT where the head lies 80–105 m above the model top (the
  water-table depth is negative: the state of the T5.5b spin-up there); on day 1 at
  −58.6°, −3.4° (7.8 m deep). These are the cells with the largest heads and river
  stages relative to their per-cell reference, where Float32 offsets round most.

**Equal closures other than tight** (same open-loop inputs):

| pair | max \|Δh\| | baseflow | capillary | distance of both to MF6 tight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GPU `:mixed` 0.01 vs MF6 0.01 | 4.4e-11 m | 3.2e-12 | 5.8e-12 | 4.2e-9 m (both) |
| GPU `:mixed` 5 vs MF6 5 | 3.5e-7 m | 2.5e-8 | 3.4e-8 | 0.51 m, baseflow 0.17 (both) |

At closure 5 both engines stop after one outer iteration per day; the heads drift up to
0.51 m from the tight heads by day 31 (−42.1°, −19.0°, Brazil, 86 m deep, RIV + DRN + EVT)
and the largest baseflow difference to tight is 17 % of the domain maximum. Both engines
make the same error there (3.5e-7 m apart), so this is the closure, not the engine.

## River-loss limit

Per record and day (RIV in layer 1), with the recorded discharge and channel volume V
(cells without an mGV cell get V = 0) and each run's own heads; 5,242,710 record-days
have a raised bottom, about 3.09 M record-days lose water:

| run | losses > 0.999 V | losses > V (created water) | max loss / V | max \|flux − formula\| / max(\|formula\|, 1e-6 C) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MF6 tight | 0 | 0 | 0.9990000 | 2.8e-7 |
| GPU `:mixed` / `:double` tight | 0 | 0 | 0.9990000 | 3.5e-7 |
| GPU `:single` tight | 128,950 | 117 (322 m³ in all) | 1.0073 | 0.33 |
| MF6 0.01 / 5, GPU `:mixed` 0.01 / 5 | 0 | 0 | 0.9990000 | ≤ 4.2e-7 |

Both engines implement the limit as the formula says (to rounding of `stage − h`). The
`:single` excess is the open issue of the T5.5b review (`river_bottom` in Float32 offsets,
`stage′ − rbot′` small against `|stage′|`); here it is 117 record-days in 31 days, 322 m³
against 22.5 km³/day of river loss. The production run uses `:mixed`.

## Timing

Wall time per daily step of MODFLOW alone (replay), **median over days 3–31** (CPU
backend: days 3–10), each stage synchronised with the device:

- **input**: setting the three fields (`set_value_device!`: gather kernels on the GPU;
  the host loops of the mf6 engine), `prepare_time_step`, writing the BOUND arrays
  (`apply_stresses!`: kernels, or libmf6's BOUND through its pointers);
- **solve**: `prepare_solve`, the outer iterations, `finalize_solve`;
- **output**: `finalize_time_step` (flows, budgets, listing file) and the coupling outputs
  (heads, baseflow, capillary rise).

Not included: reading the recorded inputs from disk and uploading them to the device
(in the coupled run they are mGV's device arrays), saving the outputs, start-up. MF6:
libmf6 6.3.0, one thread (no OpenMP/MPI in the library), in a Julia process with 8
threads. GPU engine on the CPU backend: POCL, `JULIA_KA_CPU_THREADS=16`, `julia -t 16`.

## Deviations and open issues

- MF6 here is libmf6 6.3.0 (`Modflow6_jll`, what the mf6 engine of ModflowCoupling
  loads), not the 6.8.2 build in `reference/`. The 6.8.2 build has no MPI; a parallel MF6
  needs an MPI/PETSc build (not built, as instructed). The review timed 6.8.2 (below): it
  is not faster than 6.3.0 on this model.

## Review checks (independent reviewer, 2026-11-15)

- **Parity recomputed** with an own script from the stored per-day outputs (all 3,675,450
  active nodes of both layers, all 1,713,742 land cells, all 31 days, no NaN): `:mixed`
  4.37e-11 m, baseflow 3.61e-12, capillary rise 5.32e-12; `:single` 2.52e-5 m, 1.20e-5,
  3.92e-6; as reported. `mfsim.lst` of `mf6_tight` is libmf6 6.3.0's own (7 outer / 14–15
  inner per day, run time 4 min 52 s). Both engines start from `record/heads0.nc`.
- **Closures**: both engines get the same IMS settings from one `SolverSettings` (COMPLEX,
  UNDER_RELAXATION SIMPLE 0.99, BICGSTAB, the dvcloses, INNER_RCLOSE STRICT, maxima). The
  GPU engine replaces ILUT by `mc_ilu0` and has no backtracking (MF6 never backtracked
  here: flag 0 every iteration); the Float32 floors act only in `:single`. MF6 skips the
  under-relaxation on the converged outer iteration, so at closure 5 a day is one
  unrelaxed linear solve without the nonlinear correction (outer 2 changes up to 0.044 m on
  day 1): that is the 0.51 m from tight after 31 days, in both engines alike.
- **Re-run at closure 0.01** (own replay, same record): MF6 (libmf6 6.3.0) 3881 ms per day
  (median days 3–31, range 3843–3935, no trend), GPU rocm `:mixed` 65.2 ms (64.0–66.4);
  60×. MF6 vs GPU 4.37e-11 m; MF6 0.01 vs MF6 tight 4.2e-9 m, as reported.
- **MF6 6.8.2 executable** on the written MF6 input files of closure 0.01 (period-1
  placeholders, RCH set to 0.0003 m/day; 3 outer / 5 inner per day in every run, as in the
  replay), per day from the run time of 10 minus 2 periods: 6.3.0 executable 3.68 s,
  6.8.2 4.70 s (COMPLEX as written), 6.8.2 with PRECONDITIONER_LEVELS 0 and
  BACKTRACKING_NUMBER 0 3.15 s. So the 6.3.0 executable alone matches the replay's MF6
  solve (the Julia process does not slow it), 6.8.2 is not faster, and against the fastest
  MF6 tried the GPU `:mixed` speed-up at 0.01 is about 48× (one run each, not medians).
- **River-loss limit `:single`**: own count 117 record-days, 321.5 m³, max loss/V 1.00731;
  1–7 per day without a trend. The records have V/C of 1e-2 m (median; at most 4e-2 m)
  under stages of about 1100 m (up to 4261 m): an excess of 0.7 % of V/C is 7e-5 m, the
  Float32 resolution of such elevations, so it stays a rounding effect (larger than in
  T5.5b because of the high stages).
- Not re-verified: the bitwise replay of the record run (its outputs were deleted).
- One month (31 days) of the real model, not a year; growth over the month is reported
  above.
- `:single` and the river-loss limit (T5.5b review): confirmed on the global model, 117
  record-days with losses above the channel volume in 31 days (322 m³). Not fixed here.
- The water table lies 80–105 m above the model top on some lower-Amazon cells in the
  year-200 state (where `:single` differs most); plausibility of that state not judged.
