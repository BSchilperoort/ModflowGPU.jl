# T4.4: `:mixed` preset and production default

Measured 2026-11-08 on the machine of `2026-11-07.md` (RX 7800 XT, Ryzen 7 7700, Julia
1.12.6, KernelAbstractions 0.10.5), branch `T4.4-mixed-default` (after T4.2, T4.3 and
T4.6), nothing else on the GPU (benchmark lock):

```sh
julia -t 8 --project=benchmark benchmark/run.jl rocm indus continental global5m precision=double,mixed,single timed=0 precond=jacobi,mc_ilu0,amg
JULIA_KA_CPU_THREADS=8 julia -t 8 --project=benchmark benchmark/run.jl cpu indus continental precision=double,mixed,single timed=0 precond=jacobi,mc_ilu0,amg
```

Synthetic coupling models, steady state + 30 daily steps, `synthetic_ims(:parity)` (CG,
OUTER_DVCLOSE 1e-9, INNER_DVCLOSE 1e-10; Float32 floors in `:single`). Presets:
`:double` (T = Ts = Tp = Float64), `:mixed` (T = Ts = Float64, Tp = Float32: Jacobi `minv`,
the MC-ILU0 factors, the AMG hierarchy below the finest level and its smoother data),
`:single` (all Float32). Δh: max |h − h_ref| over all cells after the steady step and
after the last step, against the `:double` **Jacobi** run of the same model (so the
`:double` rows of the other preconditioners show the closure-level difference between
preconditioners). Steady = first step; daily = mean of the 30 transient steps; total =
31 steps (no init). Every step converged. Outer iterations are identical in `:double` and
`:mixed` for every model and preconditioner; inner iterations too, except Jacobi on indus
(14292 vs 14306 steady).

## Findings

1. **`:mixed` is as accurate as `:double`.** Same outer and inner iterations, heads within
   2.3e-13–2.2e-10 m of `:double` with Jacobi and identical Δh at closure level (≤ 3.7e-9 m)
   with MC-ILU0 and AMG; the global5m steady state, the worst-conditioned case, is no
   exception (`:mixed` mc_ilu0 3.6e-9 m vs `:double` jacobi, the same as `:double`
   mc_ilu0). `:single` is 1e-6–2e-4 m away (global5m steady 2.0e-6 m with mc_ilu0).
2. **`:mixed` is faster than `:double`** where the preconditioner carries the work:
   global5m mc_ilu0 steady 4.18 → 3.36 s (−20 %), daily 0.441 → 0.386 s (−12 %), 31 steps
   17.4 → 14.9 s; continental 2.90 → 2.37 s; AMG global5m 32.5 → 27.1 s. With Jacobi the
   gain is small (−2 to −7 %, `minv` is one vector). Device memory −0.2 GiB (global5m
   mc_ilu0, −5 %) to −0.28 GiB (AMG). On CPU, mc_ilu0 `:mixed` is within ±7 % of `:double`.
3. **MC-ILU0 is the fastest preconditioner over 31 steps in every case** (both backends,
   all precisions, all sizes) and in the daily steps everywhere (Jacobi is close on small
   models: indus 0.004 vs 0.006 s). AMG is faster only in the steady state of indus and
   continental (rocm continental `:mixed` 0.68 vs 0.88 s; CPU continental 3.36 vs 6.76 s);
   on global5m rocm MC-ILU0 also wins the steady state (3.36 vs 4.52 s).
4. A per-solve choice (AMG for steady-state periods, MC-ILU0 for transient ones) would
   save 0.2 s of 2.4 s on rocm continental and 3.4 s of 17 s on CPU continental, nothing on
   global5m rocm, and would keep two preconditioners (AMG +50 % memory) in memory: not
   adopted (open option for CPU runs with long steady states).
5. Phase 4 targets with the new default (`:mixed` + mc_ilu0, rocm): global5m steady
   3.36 s (< 5 s: met), daily 0.386 s (< 0.2 s: not met; `:single` 0.167 s, < 0.1 s not
   met), steady + 30 steps 14.9 s (< 10 s: not met; `:single` 6.3 s); indus steady 0.13 s
   (< 0.35 s: met). The daily steps remain bound by per-outer work (T4.5).
